Zoning & by-law limits ▾
Every building gets its own 50 × 50 ft lot centred on it, whatever the building's own size, and lots may not overlap. A building bigger than the lot simply takes the space it needs. Dashed squares on the plan show each lot. Joinable units (square cabins, saunas, sheds, pavilions, domes) take no lot so they can still be built shoulder to shoulder, nor do fire pits, docks or parking pads. Nothing may overlap regardless. There is no cap on the number of units, place as many as the land and the lots allow. No shoreline setback is applied, but nothing can be built in the water. Confirm the real numbers with the Town of Gore Bay / NEMI zoning by-law.
