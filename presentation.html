Evergreen / Location
Manitoulin Island

The largest freshwater island in the world.

Evergreen sits on Highway 540 outside Gore Bay, on the north shore of Lake Wolsey. The parcel runs to roughly 7.5 hectares with about 259 metres of water frontage, and the internal road is already in.

Access is by the Little Current swing bridge from the north year-round, or the Chi‑Cheemaun ferry from Tobermory into South Baymouth between May and October. Sudbury is the nearest airport.

Distances and drive times are indicative and pending verification.
