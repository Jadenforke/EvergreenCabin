The opportunity

Manitoulin has the shoreline. It doesn’t have the rooms.

Manitoulin is the largest freshwater island in the world and roughly a four-hour drive from the GTA, but its accommodation stock is motels, cottages and campgrounds. Evergreen is a family-owned parcel on Lake Wolsey with 259 metres of frontage, a road already in, and a build sequenced into three phases so the first units earn while the next are engineered.

Every structure on this page is quoted, spec’d, and reviewed against the Ontario Building Code before it is drawn on the plan.

Evergreen / Location
Manitoulin Island

The largest freshwater island in the world.

Evergreen sits on Highway 540 outside Gore Bay, on the north shore of Lake Wolsey. The parcel runs to roughly 7.5 hectares with about 259 metres of water frontage, and the internal road is already in.

Access is by the Little Current swing bridge from the north year-round, or the Chi‑Cheemaun ferry from Tobermory into South Baymouth between May and October. Sudbury is the nearest airport.
